# DAT Provider Research — SEO Query & Intent Map

Updated: 2026-09-15

## Principle

Optimize around search intent, not keyword repetition. The commercial product remains a public-source Digital Asset Treasury Provider Economics Stress Test. Broad operational treasury queries can bring traffic, but the highest-value pages should own narrow decision-stage searches around provider agreements, fees, term, exit, governance, internalization and realized outcomes.

## Search-intent hierarchy

### P0 — highest commercial relevance

1. **digital asset treasury management agreement**
   - Intent: understand/review a manager contract.
   - Target page: `guide-digital-asset-treasury-management-agreement.html`
   - Supporting pages: methodology, fees guide, exit guide, case notes.

2. **digital asset treasury fees** / **digital asset treasury management fees** / **bitcoin treasury management fees**
   - Intent: compare provider compensation.
   - Target page: `guide-dat-provider-fees.html`
   - Required vocabulary: asset-based fee, AUM, denominator, performance fee, high-water mark, warrants, equity compensation, expense reimbursement.

3. **digital asset treasury termination** / **asset management agreement termination fee** / **digital asset treasury exit economics**
   - Intent: understand cost and mechanics of changing a provider.
   - Target page: `guide-dat-provider-exit-economics.html`
   - Supporting cases: CEA/10X, SharpLink, CleanCore, realized outcomes.

4. **digital asset treasury adviser agreement** / **related party digital asset treasury adviser**
   - Intent: governance and comparability review.
   - Target page: `guide-related-party-dat-agreements.html`

5. **digital asset treasury provider** / **digital asset treasury manager** / **digital asset treasury adviser**
   - Intent: category discovery and vendor/role understanding.
   - Target page: homepage + FAQ + glossary.

### P1 — useful top/mid-funnel

6. **corporate bitcoin treasury management** / **bitcoin treasury manager** / **bitcoin treasury advisor**
   - Intent is mixed between strategy, operations and outsourced management.
   - Use supporting language on the homepage and fee/agreement guides, but do not let this broaden the product into custody/accounting/software.

7. **digital asset treasury strategy** / **crypto treasury management**
   - Broad informational/operational intent. Current SERPs skew toward strategy, custody, payments, accounting and treasury software.
   - Do not make this the homepage primary keyword. Use only for context pages that clearly narrow to provider-contract economics.

### P2 — company/case long-tail

Examples:
- CEA 10X asset management agreement
- USBC Hyrcanian management fee
- SharpLink treasury manager internalization
- CleanCore treasury manager termination
- Upexi GSR asset management agreement
- Forward Industries Galaxy asset management agreement

Target with source-backed case notes and outcome pages. These queries can be low-volume but high-intent and easier to win because the competing results are often raw filings rather than decision-oriented analysis.

## Page map

- `/` → digital asset treasury provider economics; digital asset treasury manager/adviser benchmark
- `/guide-digital-asset-treasury-management-agreement.html` → digital asset treasury management agreement
- `/guide-dat-provider-fees.html` → digital asset treasury fees; bitcoin treasury management fees
- `/guide-dat-provider-exit-economics.html` → digital asset treasury termination; provider exit economics; termination fee
- `/guide-related-party-dat-agreements.html` → related-party digital asset treasury adviser agreement
- `/methodology.html` → digital asset treasury provider benchmarking methodology
- `/outcomes.html` → digital asset treasury manager renegotiation / internalization / termination outcomes
- `/faq.html` → question-form long-tail
- `/glossary.html` → term-definition long-tail
- company case pages → entity + agreement / fee / termination queries

## Avoid cannibalization

- Homepage owns the category/product phrase.
- Fee guide owns fee-intent queries.
- Exit guide owns termination/lock-in/internalization queries.
- Agreement guide owns contract-review queries.
- Related-party guide owns governance/relationship queries.
- Case notes own company-specific queries.

## Measurement

Once Search Console has enough impressions, prefer actual query data over estimated keyword tools. Track impressions, average position, CTR and landing page by query cluster. Expand pages with impressions in positions 5–30 before creating large amounts of new generic content.
