DAT Provider Economics Stress Test

Methodology

The objective is not to produce a single “market fee.” The objective is to build a reproducible factual record showing which public provider arrangements are meaningfully comparable, how current economics and exit exposure differ, what state each agreement is actually in, and what happened when similar relationships changed or ended.

1. Target agreement currentness

Version chain first

ORIGINAL → AMENDED / RESTATED → CURRENT → RENEWED / NOT RENEWED → TERMINATED / REPLACED?

We identify the executed agreement family, later amendments or restatements, current effective terms, renewal or non-renewal evidence, disclosed termination and any successor structure. A superseded or expired agreement is not used as the current target.

2. Source hierarchy

  • Primary: executed agreement, amendment, restatement, renewal / non-renewal evidence, termination, settlement or successor agreement filed with the SEC.
  • Currentness: latest 8-K, 10-Q, 10-K, proxy or other issuer filing describing present terms or provider status.
  • Governance: related-party policy, committee charter, proxy disclosure and ownership / Board relationship facts.
  • Outcome: issuer filings describing fee resets, pauses, non-payment, non-renewal, renegotiation, internalization, replacement, termination, settlement or litigation.
  • Transition controls: disclosed asset-transfer, reconciliation, accounting or approval controls arising from provider changes.
  • Secondary public materials: used only when attributable and clearly labeled as secondary.

3. Peer selection

Candidate peers are screened before deep comparison. Inclusion depends on the economic role and contract stack, not just the asset or headline percentage.

  • Discretionary asset management is separated from strategic advisory work.
  • Staking, custody, derivatives, treasury execution and capital-markets services are identified separately.
  • Related-party and unrelated-party structures are tagged explicitly.
  • Asset type, AUM scale, payment denominator and fee mechanics are considered.
  • Material non-comparability is disclosed rather than averaged away.

4. Economics and exit normalization

ROLE / SCOPE · AUM / DENOMINATOR · BASE FEE · PERFORMANCE FEE · HURDLE / HWM · MINIMUMS · EQUITY / WARRANTS · PAYMENT MEDIUM · TERM · RENEWAL / NON-RENEWAL · COMPANY TERMINATION · PROVIDER TERMINATION · NOTICE / CURE · LIQUIDATED DAMAGES / ACCELERATION · RELATED-PARTY STATUS · GOVERNANCE RIGHTS · AMENDMENTS · FEE STATUS · REALIZED OUTCOME · INTERNALIZATION / REPLACEMENT · TRANSITION CONTROLS

Where the contract permits it, remaining-term or liquidated-damages exposure is shown separately from recurring fee economics instead of being hidden inside a single percentage comparison.

5. Provider-state taxonomy

Agreement status is not reduced to a binary “active / terminated” field. Public records can show materially different intermediate states, so the stress test records them separately:

SIGNED / NOT YET FUNDED ACTIVE / FUNDED ACTIVE — FEE PAUSED OR WAIVED ACTIVE — ACCRUING / UNPAID EXPIRED / NOT RENEWED STRATEGY ENDED — CONTRACT STATUS UNRESOLVED TERMINATED — EXPOSURE UNSETTLED TERMINATED — SETTLED REPLACED / INTERNALIZED

A fee pause does not by itself terminate the agreement. Zero funded assets do not by themselves prove termination. Non-payment does not by itself stop accrual. Expiration / non-renewal is distinct from early termination and may have different economics. A terminated contract may still have unresolved settlement or surviving-rights questions. The label used in a client pack is therefore tied to the latest primary-source evidence rather than inferred from strategy headlines.

6. Realized outcome controls

Public outcomes are used as controls, not as predictions. The stress test records observable fee resets, pauses, unpaid accruals, non-renewals, internalizations, negotiated exits, settlements, litigation and replacement structures. This can show that similarly priced agreements produced very different lifecycle economics — or that a theoretically heavy exit clause was actually renegotiated into a different result.

7. What the pack can and cannot say

The pack may state that a provision sits above, below or within an observable public range, that a contract stack differs materially from selected public comparables, or that public precedents show particular renegotiation / exit outcomes.

It does not state that a contract is fair, unfair, lawful, unlawful, fiduciary-compliant, accounting-compliant or arm's-length. Those are conclusions for the client and its legal / financial / accounting advisers.

8. QA before delivery

  • Current contract version and provider status rechecked.
  • Renewal / non-renewal status rechecked separately from termination.
  • Funded-asset, strategy, fee-accrual and payment status rechecked separately.
  • Peer inclusion logic rechecked.
  • Fee formulas, hurdle / HWM mechanics and annualized examples recalculated.
  • Termination / remaining-term mechanics reconciled to executed language where available.
  • Material related-party, governance and outcome facts reconciled to primary sources.
  • Every material factual claim linked to a source.
  • Unknowns and unavailable information listed explicitly.

Methodology is designed for public-source factual research and may be adapted to a matter's scope. It is not a legal, fairness, fiduciary, accounting or investment methodology.
