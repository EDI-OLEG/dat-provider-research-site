Case note · Solmate / strategic advisers + Pulsar

Solmate: a 10-year SOL-AUM adviser agreement reaches its first annual fee window while a separate $250k/month related-party advisory fee remains paused, not terminated.

Brera Holdings PLC, operating publicly as Solmate Infrastructure, provides a useful agreement-family currentness case. Its September 2025 strategic-adviser contract combines an annual SOL-AUM fee with multiple warrant layers and a ten-year term. A separate 2026 Pulsar Group advisory agreement charged $250,000 per month before payment and accrual were suspended in April while the rest of that agreement remained in force.

The first annual SOL-AUM fee becomes a September 2026 currentness event

The strategic-adviser agreement does not use a monthly AUM charge. The advisers' cash compensation is calculated annually around the September anniversary, with SOL AUM valued using the 15-calendar-day time-weighted average price specified in the agreement. The amended 2026 annual-report record expressly says the first anniversary occurs in September 2026.

The same annual-report record reported only €237 of accrued SOL-AUM strategic-advisory fee for 2025, while August 2026 company disclosures reported approximately 1.25 million SOL and an estimated market value above $100 million at the cited market price. Those facts do not establish the exact September fee: the agreement's anniversary-date TWAP and then-current SOL AUM control the calculation. They do show why the anniversary is now a live fee-measurement event rather than a historical footnote.

Currentness rule: for anniversary-based compensation, holdings today do not equal the payable fee. Freeze the measurement date, exact AUM definition, valuation method, agreement status and any later waiver or amendment before dollarizing exposure.

The warrant package is economically separate from the annual cash fee

The filed agreement grants three warrant families in addition to the annual SOL-AUM advisory share. The pre-funded warrants are described as fully earned at the Effective Date and not subject to revocation or clawback if the company later terminates an adviser. Performance-based common warrants become fully earned when the applicable performance metric is achieved and likewise are not subject to revocation or clawback after they are earned.

This makes Solmate a useful noncash-compensation control: the economic record must separate recurring AUM-linked cash compensation from already-earned or performance-earned equity rights that can survive a later change in the advisory relationship.

The adviser relationship later overlapped with governance and management

At execution, the strategic advisers were not directors. Later issuer disclosures state that four strategic advisers joined the Board, and 2026 disclosures also state that two of the advisers subsequently became Interim Chief Operating Officer and Chief Executive Officer. These are relationship and governance facts; they do not by themselves establish whether the original economics were fair or unfair.

Pulsar shows why “fee stopped” and “agreement terminated” are different states

The separate Pulsar Group agreement covered exclusive advisory support for business development and blockchain / technology operations across the Gulf Cooperation Council region. The fee was $250,000 per month, retroactive to January 1, 2026. On April 24, 2026, the parties suspended both payment and accrual of that monthly fee until further notice as part of a cost-reduction initiative.

The public filing expressly states that the other terms remained in full force and effect. That is not the same state as termination. For provider-economics research, ACTIVE + FEE PAUSED must remain distinct from TERMINATED.

The strategic-adviser contract also has an operational-currentness wrinkle

An August 2026 registration statement continued to describe the Strategic Advisor Agreement and its compensation structure, but also said that the company did not then plan to pursue initiatives regarding its SOL treasury pursuant to that agreement. Separately, August 2026 company disclosures reported continued SOL accumulation and active blockchain / staking expansion.

Those statements are not necessarily contradictory: treasury activity can continue while a particular adviser agreement is not being used for new treasury initiatives. The correct research treatment is therefore agreement-specific currentness, not an assumption that an active token treasury automatically means every adviser layer is operationally active.

Why this case matters for a Board / CFO stress test

Solmate combines four recurring diligence problems in one public record: anniversary-based AUM compensation, large separate warrant economics, advisers who later overlap with Board / executive roles, and a second related-party advisory agreement whose fee was suspended without terminating the contract. It is a strong control for separating provider status, fee status and governance status.
