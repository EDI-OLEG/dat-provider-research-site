How a Provider Economics Stress Test works

The standard scope is intentionally narrow: one target provider agreement family, a verified public peer set, explicit exit math, realized outcome controls and a source-linked factual record. No data-room access is required for the standard engagement.

Standard scope

What you provide

  • Target company / provider
  • Decision or question being reviewed
  • Known agreement date or filing, if available
  • Any public peers you want considered

The standard engagement does not require confidential client documents or credentials.

What you receive

  • 1-page decision evidence summary
  • Current contract / amendment ledger
  • 5–7 verified primary comparables
  • Scope-normalized recurring / performance economics
  • Term, renewal, termination and liquidated-damages analysis
  • Relevant renegotiation / internalization / exit / litigation controls
  • Related-party / governance facts and transition-control signals where public
  • Source ledger, unknowns and non-comparability notes

Workflow

1
Conflict / scope check.
We confirm the target, economic parties and intended use, and disclose any known relationship that could affect independence.
2
Current-version verification.
Executed agreement, amendments / restatements, latest issuer disclosures and termination status are reconciled before benchmarking.
3
Economics and exit normalization.
Fees, denominators, hurdles, warrants / equity, term, renewal and company/provider termination rights are normalized before comparison. Remaining-term or liquidated-damages exposure is shown explicitly where the public contract permits it.
4
Outcome and transition review.
Where public evidence exists, we capture fee resets, internalizations, replacements, terminations, settlements, litigation and any disclosed asset-transfer / reconciliation control consequences.
5
QA and delivery.
Currentness, peer logic, formulas, material factual claims and links receive a final check. One revision is included in the founding scope.

Data and security posture

  • Public-source by default. SEC filings, executed public agreements, issuer disclosures and attributable public materials.
  • No client system access required. No mailbox, data room or internal document access is needed for the standard product.
  • No tracking on this site. The static site uses no analytics, cookies, tracking pixels or JavaScript dependencies.
  • No privilege promise. If counsel retains the service for a matter, privilege / work-product treatment is for counsel to determine; the product itself is factual public-source research.
