Illustrative finding

A provider fee is only one layer of the decision. The economically material comparison may be the combined stack: recurring and performance compensation, contract duration, unilateral exit rights, remaining-term / liquidated-damages exposure, equity consideration, related-party facts, internalization alternatives and what actually happened when comparable arrangements changed or ended.

What a 72-hour stress test adds

  • Verifies the current contract version before comparison.
  • Separates unlike provider roles instead of averaging them into a false market rate.
  • Normalizes fee denominator, performance hurdle and equity / warrant economics.
  • Maps company-side and provider-side termination rights and disclosed exit math.
  • Adds realized renegotiation, internalization, termination, settlement and litigation controls.
  • Flags transition / asset-transfer control signals where the public record contains them.

No conclusion is expressed here about fairness, legality, fiduciary compliance, accounting treatment or arm's-length status. Public sample for methodology demonstration only.
