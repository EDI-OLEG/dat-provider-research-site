Illustrative finding

A 2% fee, standing alone, is not enough to establish an outlier. Public DAT arrangements include fee levels and long terms in a similar broad range. The more decision-relevant comparison may be the combined stack: scope, related-party status, term, unilateral exit rights, acceleration / liquidated damages, equity consideration and observed outcomes in similar structures.

Why that matters

A benchmark that only says “peer median fee = X%” can create false confidence. A decision-maker may need to know that an apparently ordinary fee sits inside a contract with materially different lock-in or conflict economics — or, conversely, that an activist narrative about the fee is not supported by public comparables.
