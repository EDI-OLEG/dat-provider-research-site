Illustrative finding

A market-rate answer can be wrong even when every percentage in the table is copied correctly. The decision-relevant record is the combined stack: provider role, fee denominator, payment medium, equity / warrant consideration, contract duration, ordinary exit path, remaining-term economics, current accrued / paid status and what happened when comparable relationships changed or ended.

What the 72-hour stress test adds

  • Current-version verification: executed agreement, amendments, latest filing and termination state before comparison.
  • Role normalization: discretionary manager, strategic adviser, staking provider and services layer are not averaged into one false peer rate.
  • Fee normalization: denominator, measurement date, minimums, performance mechanics and cash versus equity settlement.
  • Exit reconstruction: company/provider rights, notice, voting or performance gates, remaining-term formulas and realized settlement.
  • Currentness controls: effective fee schedule versus later accrual / invoice / payment / recognized expense; active, paused, waived, unpaid, expired and terminated remain distinct states.
  • Outcome controls: fee resets, internalization, non-renewal, settlements, disputes and provider replacement structures.
