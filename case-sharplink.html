Sharplink: external management to internal capability.

Public-source case note. Factual research only.

Launch

On May 30, 2025 Sharplink entered separate discretionary ETH asset-management agreements with Galaxy Digital Capital Management and ParaFi Capital. Public filings describe tiered asset-based fees of 0.25% to 1.25% per year and a $1.25 million annual minimum for each manager.

Exit

On April 3, 2026 Sharplink signed mutual termination agreements with both managers, effective May 31, 2026. The company said the decision reflected its evolution, including internal asset-management personnel, and disclosed no termination fees or penalties.

Later structure

In August 2026 Sharplink and Galaxy launched a separate $125 million onchain yield fund. Galaxy serves as investment manager and Sharplink participates as a limited partner.

Benchmark lesson

A useful DAT comparison should include external management versus internalization, not just manager-versus-manager pricing. Minimum fees, exit rights and later replacement structures belong in the evidence record.

No legal, fairness, fiduciary or investment opinion.
